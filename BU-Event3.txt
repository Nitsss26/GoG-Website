# Inauguration of Training Programme  

The Two-Day Training Programme on "Green Energy" was organized by the Department of Electrical Engineering, Barkatullah University, under the scheme of Pradhan Mantri Uchchatar Shiksha Abhiyan from 23–24 February 2026.
The inaugural session was graced by Prof. S.K. Jain, Hon’ble Vice-Chancellor, and Prof. Vipin Vyas, Nodal Officer, PM-UShA, as Chief Guests. Dr Neeraj Gaur, Director, UIT, and Dr Taruna Jain, Head of the Department of Electrical Engineering, welcomed the dignitaries and highlighted the importance of green energy and sustainable development.
The programme focused on renewable energy technologies, sustainability, and capacity building. The dignitaries appreciated the department’s initiative and encouraged participants to actively contribute towards innovation in the field of green energy.

# Theme and Relevance of the Workshop

The central theme of this National Workshop on "Green Energy" is to explore, promote, and implement sustainable energy technologies that can help India transition from conventional fossil fuel–based systems to clean, renewable, and environmentally responsible energy sources.
“Green energy” refers to energy derived from natural resources such as sunlight, wind, water, biomass, and geothermal heat that are naturally replenished and cause minimal environmental harm. In today’s world, where climate change, global warming, and pollution are growing concerns, green energy is not just an alternative — it is a necessity.

# Alignment with the Academic Curriculum

The National Workshop on "Green Energy" was well aligned with the academic curriculum of the Department of Electrical Engineering at UIT, Barkatullah University. The workshop topics complemented core subjects such as renewable energy systems, power electronics, energy conversion, and sustainable power generation.

The sessions helped students connect theoretical classroom concepts with practical applications through real-world examples, case studies, and discussions on emerging green energy technologies. It also supported project work, laboratory learning, and research activities.

Conducted under the PM-UShA scheme, the workshop strengthened outcome-based learning and enhanced students’ technical understanding, sustainability awareness, and industry readiness.
