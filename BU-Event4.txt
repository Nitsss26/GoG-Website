# Inauguration of Training Programme  

The Two-Day Training Program on "Career Guidance and Placement" began with a formal inauguration organized by the Department of Sociology, Barkatullah University, Bhopal under the PM-UShA Scheme.

The Chief Guests for the inaugural session were Dr. Dhirendra Chaturvedi, EC Member of Barkatullah University, Bhopal, and Prof. Anita Dhurvey, Head of the Department of Sociology.

In their addresses, they highlighted the importance of career planning, skill development, and active participation in such programs to enhance academic and professional growth. The session concluded with motivating words encouraging students to take full advantage of the training.

# Relevance to the PM-UShA Scheme


The Training Program on "Career Guidance and Placement", organized by the Department of Sociology, Barkatullah University, Bhopal, was closely aligned with the objectives of the PM-UShA Scheme. Its relevance can be understood through the following points:
Enhancement of Quality Education: The program promoted career-oriented learning alongside academic knowledge.
Focus on Employability: It emphasized skill development, placement preparation, and workplace readiness.
Institutional Strengthening: It contributed to improving the academic and professional support system within the university.
Holistic Student Development: The workshop encouraged structured career planning and long-term academic progression.
Bridging Academia and Industry: It reduced the gap between classroom learning and real-world career opportunities.

Thus, the program effectively supported the broader mission of the PM-UShA Scheme in strengthening higher education and student career outcomes.

# Objectives of the Workshop

The main objectives of the Training Program on "Career Guidance and Placement", organised by the Department of Sociology, Barkatullah University, Bhopal, under the PM-UShA Scheme, were as follows:

To provide structured guidance for career planning and goal setting.
To create awareness about higher education opportunities and research pathways.
To inform students about competitive examinations and job prospects in various sectors.
To enhance employability skills such as communication, resume writing, and interview preparation.
To develop confidence and understanding of workplace culture and professional ethics.
To bridge the gap between academic knowledge and practical career opportunities.

The workshop aimed to empower students with clarity, direction, and readiness for their future careers.

# Hands-on Training & Practical  Exposure

The Two-Day Training Program on "Career Guidance and Placement", organised by the Department of Sociology, Barkatullah University, Bhopal, under the PM-UShA Scheme, provided practical exposure through interactive and guidance-based sessions rather than formal activity-based workshops.
The practical exposure included:
Interactive Guidance Sessions: Students directly interacted with experts and clarified their doubts regarding career options and higher studies.
Real-Life Career Insights: Resource persons shared practical experiences and examples from academic and professional fields.
Question & Answer Sessions: Students asked queries related to competitive exams, job opportunities, and skill requirements.
These sessions ensured practical understanding and clarity, helping participants connect theoretical knowledge with real-world career pathways.

# Outcomes and Learning Impact

The Training Program on "Career Guidance and Placement", organised by the Department of Sociology, Barkatullah University, Bhopal, under the PM-UShA Scheme, had a positive learning impact on the participants.

Students gained better clarity about career options, higher education pathways, and placement opportunities. The program increased their confidence, enhanced their awareness about professional requirements, and motivated them to focus on skill development. Overall, it helped participants become more prepared and focused on their academic and professional goals.

# Theme and Relevance of the Workshop

The theme of the Two-Day Training Program on "Career Guidance and Placement" focused on helping students develop structured career plans and improve their employability skills. Organized by the Department of Sociology, Barkatullah University, Bhopal under the PM-UShA Scheme, the workshop aimed to connect academic learning with real career opportunities.

# Alignment with the Academic Curriculum

The Training Program on "Career Guidance and Placement" was closely aligned with the academic curriculum of the Department of Sociology, Barkatullah University, Bhopal. The sessions complemented classroom learning by connecting theoretical knowledge with practical career opportunities and professional skills.

The program supported students in understanding how their academic subjects relate to real-world careers, research fields, competitive examinations, and higher studies. It enhanced their awareness of academic progression, workplace expectations, and skill development, thereby strengthening the practical relevance of their curriculum.
