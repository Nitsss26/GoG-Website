# Inauguration of Training Programme  

The training programme was formally inaugurated at the Department of Mathematics, Barkatullah University, Bhopal, under the PM–UShA Scheme. The inaugural session was graced by the presence of the Chief Guests Registrar Dr. Samar Bahadur Singh, Prof. Neeraj Gour and Prof. Ragini Gothalwal, whose presence added academic value and encouragement to the programme. The session was presided over by Dr. Garima Singh, Head of the Department of Mathematics, who welcomed the guests and participants.
In their inaugural addresses, the dignitaries highlighted the importance of integrating Indian Knowledge Systems with modern education and emphasized the role of such training programmes in strengthening academic foundations and interdisciplinary learning. The session set a positive and purposeful tone for the training programme, motivating participants to actively engage in the sessions that followed.

# Relevance to the PM-UShA Scheme


The workshop on Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) was closely aligned with the objectives of the PM–UShA (Pradhan Mantri Uchchatar Shiksha Abhiyan) Scheme, which emphasizes improving the quality, relevance, and outcomes of higher education through multidisciplinary learning, skill development, and innovation. By integrating traditional Indian knowledge with modern academic and technological perspectives, the workshop contributed to holistic and value-based education envisioned under the scheme.

Enhanced institutional academic enrichment through workshops, faculty–student interaction, and knowledge dissemination activities
Fostered cultural rootedness and national heritage, aligning with PM–UShA’s focus on Indian ethos in higher education
Promoted multidisciplinary and holistic education by integrating IKS with engineering, neuroscience, research, and management studies
Supported sustainability and societal relevance


# Objectives of the Workshop

The key objectives of workshop were :

To create awareness about Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) and their contemporary relevance
To integrate ancient Indian wisdom with modern education, science, and technology
To highlight indigenous scientific methods and research approaches
Develop analytical and critical thinking skills
To foster cultural awareness and sustainable development perspectives

# Hands-on Training & Practical  Exposure

The workshop provided meaningful hands-on training and practical exposure to participants by encouraging the application of Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) concepts in real-world and academic contexts. Interactive sessions, case discussions, and activity-based learning enabled participants to move beyond theoretical understanding and engage with IKS in a practical and experiential manner.

Exposure to real-life applications of IKS in ethics, governance, innovation, and decision-making
Case-based discussions linking ancient Indian frameworks with modern scientific and technological challenges
Interactive activities to demonstrate interdisciplinary applications of IKS in mathematics, engineering, and humanities
Engagement in innovation and entrepreneurship-oriented discussions rooted in indigenous knowledge

Participants developed a better understanding of IKS and its real-world relevance
Improved awareness of ethical values and responsible thinking

# Theme and Relevance of the Workshop

The workshop focused on the theme of Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) as a convergence of ancient wisdom and modern innovation, emphasizing their relevance in ethical governance, engineering, research, and entrepreneurship. Through concepts such as Neetishastra, Rasa theory, and ancient scientific methodologies, the programme highlighted the practical applicability of traditional knowledge in contemporary contexts.
The workshop was relevant in showcasing how IKS can complement modern science and technology by promoting ethical leadership, critical thinking, creativity, and sustainable innovation. By integrating heritage knowledge with present-day challenges, the programme encouraged participants to adopt interdisciplinary and culturally rooted approaches in academics, research, and professional practice.

# Alignment with the Academic Curriculum

The workshop was thoughtfully aligned with the academic curriculum, enhancing classroom learning by integrating Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) with contemporary subjects across humanities, science, engineering, and management disciplines. It supported value-based education by connecting traditional ethical frameworks and indigenous scientific approaches with modern academic theories, thereby enriching students’ conceptual understanding and interdisciplinary perspective.

Reinforced ethical studies and value education through concepts of Neetishastra and Rajneeti
Supported research methodology courses through discussions on ancient scientific methods and inquiry-based learning
Encouraged interdisciplinary learning consistent with contemporary higher education frameworks
