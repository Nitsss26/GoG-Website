# Theme and Relevance of the Workshop

The central theme of the Faculty Development Program (FDP) on “Soft Skills” is to strengthen the professional and interpersonal competencies of faculty members in contemporary academic settings. Soft skills include essential abilities such as effective communication, teamwork, leadership, adaptability, and professional etiquette, which enable educators to engage more effectively with students and colleagues.
In the rapidly evolving landscape of higher education, the relevance of soft skills has grown significantly, as they contribute to improved teaching effectiveness, collaborative learning environments, and holistic faculty development. The program therefore aims to support educators in enhancing their communication and professional capabilities, thereby contributing to overall academic excellence.


# Alignment with the Academic Curriculum

The Faculty Development Program on “Soft Skills” was well aligned with the academic and professional development objectives of higher education institutions. The sessions complemented essential aspects of teaching and learning such as effective communication, classroom management, professional ethics, and collaborative engagement.
The program helped participants connect theoretical perspectives of communication and interpersonal behaviour with their practical application in academic environments through interactive discussions, activities, and real-life examples. It also supported teaching effectiveness, faculty development, and improved academic interaction with students.
Conducted in alignment with PM-UShA and NEP 2020 objectives, the program strengthened outcome-based learning and professional competency development, thereby enhancing faculty preparedness and overall academic quality.
