# Hands-on Training & Practical  Exposure

#Theme and Relevance of the Workshop

The workshop aimed to integrate modern AI-based tools with traditional Indian ecological knowledge to develop a comprehensive understanding of environmental challenges and sustainable solutions.

The relevance of the workshop lies in addressing contemporary issues such as climate change, resource management, biodiversity conservation, and disaster resilience. While artificial intelligence provides advanced capabilities for data analysis, prediction, and visualization, Indian Knowledge Systems contribute ethical, sustainable, and culturally rooted perspectives. Through conceptual discussions and hands-on activities, the workshop enabled participants to understand and apply these integrated approaches in real-world environmental contexts, making it highly relevant for students and researchers in environmental science.



#Alignment with the Academic Curriculum

The workshop was closely aligned with the NEP-2020 and academic curriculum of the Department of Environmental Sciences and Limnology, Barkatullah University, complementing core subjects by integrating contemporary AI applications with environmental and ecological studies. It strengthened conceptual understanding while promoting practical and interdisciplinary learning.

Bridged theory with practice through AI-based environmental applications
Encouraged interdisciplinary thinking across life sciences and technology
Supported experiential learning via hands-on activities and case discussions

#Training Module & Content

Event Flow with Timeline					
Event : Application of AI (Artifical Intelligence) and role of IKS in Environmental Science				Department Name : Environmental Sciences and Limnology	
Module Wise Topics					
Module	Day	Duration	Topics	Learning Objectives	
Module 1	Day 1 (Tuesday)	120	Understanding the Planet: Past Wisdom and Present Challenges	Understand environmental challenges by integrating modern science with ancient Indian ecological wisdom.	
Module 2	Day 1 (Tuesday)	120	Demystifying AI: How Intelligent Systems Engage with Nature	"Develop a clear, non‑technical understanding of AI and its role in addressing environmental issues."	
Module 3	Day 2 (Wednesday)	120	"AI for Earth Systems: Climate, Water, and Food Security"	"Explore how AI supports climate prediction, water management, and sustainable agriculture."	
Module 4	Day 2 (Wednesday)	120	"Ancient Roots, Modern Intelligence: IKS Meets AI"	Appreciate the relevance of Indian Knowledge Systems and their integration with modern AI tools.	
Module 5	Day 3 (Thursday)	120	Watching the Earth Live: AI‑Driven Environmental Insights	Gain hands‑on experience in visually analyzing real‑world environmental data using AI platforms.	
Module 6 (Hands On)	Day 3 (Thursday)	120	Connecting Knowledge to Conservation	Consolidate foundational concepts and understand AI applications in biodiversity conservation.	
Module 7	Day 4 (Friday)	120	"Risk, Responsibility, and Resilience in the Age of AI"	Understand the role of AI in disaster management along with ethical and sustainability considerations.	
Module 8 (Hands On)	Day 4 (Friday)	120	From Ideas to Impact: Designing Solutions for Nature	Apply AI and IKS concepts collaboratively to design conceptual solutions for environmental problems.	
Module 9	Day 5 (Saturday)	120	"Purpose, Pathways, and Perspectives"	"Explore future academic, career, and societal pathways in AI‑enabled environmental science."	
Module 10 (Revision & valedictory)	Day 5 (Saturday)	120	"Integrating It All: Reflection, Revision, and the Road Ahead"	"Synthesize all learning outcomes to form a holistic perspective on AI, IKS, and environmental sustainability."
