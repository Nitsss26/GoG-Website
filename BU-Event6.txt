# Inauguration of Training Programme  

The International Seminar on “Education, Employment and Entrepreneurship” was organized by the Department of Continuing Education, Barkatullah University, under the Pradhan Mantri Uchchatar Shiksha Abhiyan (PM-UShA) scheme.
The inaugural session was graced by senior university dignitaries, including the Hon’ble Registrar and, along with Deans and Heads of various departments. The speakers emphasised the growing significance of aligning higher education with employability and entrepreneurial development in a globalised knowledge economy.
The dignitaries appreciated the department’s initiative in fostering international academic dialogue and encouraged participants to actively engage in research, innovation, and skill-oriented learning.

# Relevance to the PM-UShA Scheme

The International Seminar on “Education, Employment and Entrepreneurship” strongly aligned with the objectives of PM-UShA, which seeks to enhance the quality, inclusivity, and employability focus of higher education institutions.
Key points of relevance include;

Promoted skill-oriented and lifelong learning approaches
Strengthened employability and entrepreneurial awareness
Encouraged research, innovation, and global academic collaboration
Supported inclusive participation, including PwD representation
Enhanced institutional capacity through international engagement
Overall, the seminar effectively advanced the PM-UShA mission of fostering outcome-based, industry-relevant, and globally connected higher education.

# Objectives of the Workshop

The International Seminar on “Education, Employment and Entrepreneurship” was organized with the following key objectives;

To examine the interconnection between education, employability, and entrepreneurship in the contemporary global context
To promote skill-based, outcome-oriented, and lifelong learning approaches
To bridge the gap between academic knowledge and industry requirements
To encourage research, innovation, and interdisciplinary collaboration
To provide a platform for international academic exchange and knowledge sharing
To enhance entrepreneurial thinking and career readiness among participants
To contribute to the goals of the PM-UShA scheme through quality-driven academic engagement

# Theme and Relevance of the Workshop

The central theme of the International Seminar on “Education, Employment and Entrepreneurship” was to examine the evolving relationship between higher education, workforce readiness, and entrepreneurial development in a rapidly transforming global economy. The seminar aimed to explore innovative educational models, skill-based learning approaches, and sustainable employment strategies that empower individuals to become job creators rather than job seekers.
In the contemporary context of technological disruption, shifting industry demands, and global competition, the integration of education with employability and entrepreneurship is no longer optional — it is essential for national growth and inclusive development.

# Alignment with the Academic Curriculum

The International Seminar on “Education, Employment and Entrepreneurship” was closely aligned with the academic and extension objectives of the Department of Continuing Education at Barkatullah University. The seminar themes complemented subjects related to lifelong learning, skill development, vocational training, and community-oriented education.
The sessions enabled participants to connect theoretical perspectives on education and employment with practical insights through research presentations and policy discussions. Conducted under the PM-UShA scheme, the seminar strengthened outcome-based learning, enhanced employability awareness, and promoted entrepreneurial thinking in alignment with contemporary higher education goals.
